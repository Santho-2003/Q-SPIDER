SPOTSTAR - SURYA MOVIES

Suriya, also known as Saravanan Sivakumar, is a popular Indian actor in Tamil cinema. Known for his versatile acting skills,
he has starred in a variety of roles in films like *Ghajini*, *Singam*, and *Soorarai Pottru*.
Suriya has won several awards, including Filmfare and National Film Awards, for his performances.
He is also involved in philanthropic activities through his Agaram Foundation, which focuses on education for underprivileged children.
Suriya's dedication to both his craft and social causes has made him a beloved figure in Indian cinema.
