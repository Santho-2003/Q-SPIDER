SPOTSTAR - THERI MOVIE

Theri (2016) is a Tamil action drama directed by Atlee and stars Vijay in a powerful role.
The film follows the story of a retired police officer, Vijay, who leads a peaceful life with his daughter
but is drawn back into action to confront his past enemies.
The narrative combines high-stakes action with emotional drama, showcasing Vijay’s dual role as a loving father and a formidable cop.

Rating: 4.9/5
