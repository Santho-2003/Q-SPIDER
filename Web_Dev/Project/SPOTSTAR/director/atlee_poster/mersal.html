SPOTSTAR - MERSAL MOVIE

Mersal (2017) is a Tamil action thriller directed by Atlee and starring Vijay in a triple role.
The film centers on Vetri, a doctor who seeks to expose corruption in the medical industry, and his efforts to bring justice.
Vijay's performances, including his portrayal of three distinct characters, were widely praised.
The movie combines action, drama, and social commentary, highlighting issues like healthcare corruption and societal inequality.
Mersal was a major commercial success, known for its engaging storyline, vibrant music by A.R. Rahman, and impressive visual effects.

Rating: 4.9/5
