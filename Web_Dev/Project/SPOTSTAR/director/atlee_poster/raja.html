SPOTSTAR - RAJA MOVIE

Raja Rani (2013) is a Tamil romantic drama directed by Atlee, featuring Arya, Nayanthara, Jai, and Nazriya Nazim in leading roles.
The film explores the complexities of love and relationships through the lives of two individuals, John and Regina, who enter into an arranged marriage but struggle with their past relationships.
The narrative delves into their emotional journey, revealing how they come to understand and support each other.

Rating: 4.9/5
