SPOTSTAR - BIGIL MOVIE

Bigil (2019) is a Tamil sports action drama directed by Atlee, starring Vijay in dual roles as Michael, a gangster, and his father Rayappan.
The story follows Michael, also known as Bigil, who is a former football player turned coach.
He takes on the challenge of leading a women’s football team to victory, fulfilling his father's dream and empowering the players.
The film blends action, sports, and social themes, highlighting women's empowerment and overcoming adversity.
Bigil was a commercial success, praised for Vijay's performance, the emotional depth of the story, and the rousing football sequences.

Rating: 4.9/5
