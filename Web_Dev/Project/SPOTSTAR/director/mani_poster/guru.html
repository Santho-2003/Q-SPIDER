SPOTSTAR - GURU MOVIE

Guru (2007) is a biographical drama directed by Mani Ratnam, starring Abhishek Bachchan and Aishwarya Rai.
The film is inspired by the life of Indian business tycoon Dhirubhai Ambani and
follows the rise of a determined entrepreneur, Gurukant Desai, as he overcomes challenges to build a business empire.
Praised for its powerful performances, particularly by Abhishek Bachchan,
and A.R. Rahman's memorable soundtrack, Guru became a critical and commercial success.

Rating: 4.9/5
