SPOTSTAR - KAATRU MOVIE

Kaatru Veliyidai (2017) is a Tamil romantic drama directed by Mani Ratnam, starring Karthi and Aditi Rao Hydari.
The film tells the intense love story between a fighter pilot and a doctor, set against the backdrop of the Kargil War, exploring themes of love, conflict, and redemption.
Known for its breathtaking visuals, strong performances, and A.R. Rahman's evocative music, Kaatru Veliyidai received praise for its poetic storytelling and cinematography.
