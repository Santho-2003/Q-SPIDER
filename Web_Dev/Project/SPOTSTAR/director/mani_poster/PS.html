SPOTSTAR - PONNIN SELVAN MOVIE

Ponniyin Selvan: Part 1 (2022) is a Tamil epic historical drama directed by Mani Ratnam, based on Kalki Krishnamurthy's classic novel.
The film follows the political intrigue, power struggles, and epic battles in the Chola dynasty,
with a star-studded cast including Vikram, Aishwarya Rai, and Karthi.
Praised for its grand visuals, compelling performances, and faithful adaptation, PS-1 became a major success and a landmark in Indian cinema..

Rating: 4.9/5
