SPOTSTAR - RAAYAN MOVIE

Raayan is a Tamil film directed by Mani, starring Srikanth in the lead role.
The movie is an action drama that revolves around a young man who fights against the odds to protect his family and uphold justice.
With its mix of intense action sequences and emotional storytelling, Raayan garnered attention for Srikanth's performance and its engaging narrative.

Rating: 4.9/5
