SPOTSTAR - THALAPATHY MOVIE

Thalapathi (1991) is a Tamil crime drama directed by Mani Ratnam, starring Rajinikanth and Mammootty in lead roles.
The film is inspired by the friendship between Karna and Duryodhana from the Mahabharata and follows Surya (Rajinikanth),
an orphan raised in the slums who becomes a powerful figure in the underworld, forming a deep bond with a don, Deva (Mammootty).
Known for its intense performances, powerful storytelling, and Ilaiyaraaja's memorable music, Thalapathi is considered a classic in Indian cinema.
