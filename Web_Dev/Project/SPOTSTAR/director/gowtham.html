SPOTSTAR GOWTHAM MOVIES

Gautham Vasudev Menon is a prominent Indian filmmaker, screenwriter, and producer known for his work primarily in Tamil cinema.
He is celebrated for his unique storytelling style, often blending romance, action, and drama, with films like Vinnaithaandi Varuvaayaa, Kaakha Kaakha, and Vettaiyaadu Vilaiyaadu.
Gautham Menon has earned critical acclaim for his ability to create emotionally resonant characters and compelling narratives.
