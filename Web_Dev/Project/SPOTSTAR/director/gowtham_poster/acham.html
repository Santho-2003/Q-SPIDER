SPOTSTAR - ACHAM MOVIE

Acham Thavir (2009) is a Tamil crime thriller directed by R.K. Selvamani, featuring Suriya in the lead role.
The film revolves around a police officer who is determined to solve a complex case involving a series of murders and corruption.
Suriya's performance as the dedicated and tough cop was well-received, adding depth to the film's gripping narrative.
The movie blends intense action with suspenseful elements, keeping the audience engaged throughout.
Acham Thavir was appreciated for its compelling storyline and Suriya's strong portrayal of the protagonist.

Rating: 4.8/5
