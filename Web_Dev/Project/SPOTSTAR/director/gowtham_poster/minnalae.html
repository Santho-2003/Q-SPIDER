SPOTSTAR - MINNALAE MOVIE

Minnale (2001) is a Tamil romantic drama directed by Gautham Menon, starring R. Madhavan and Reemma Sen.
The film tells the story of a young man, Ram, who falls in love with a woman, but his attempts to win her over are complicated by his own deception.
Ram initially poses as someone else to win her affection, leading to a web of lies and emotional turmoil.
The film is known for its engaging storyline, melodious music by Harris Jayaraj, and the chemistry between the lead actors.
Minnale was well-received for its fresh approach to romance and its memorable soundtrack.

Rating: 4.7/5
