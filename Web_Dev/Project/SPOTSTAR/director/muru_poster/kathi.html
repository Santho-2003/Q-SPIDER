SPOTSTAR - KATHI MOVIE

"Kaththi" is a 2014 Tamil action drama directed by A.R. Murugadoss, starring Vijay in dual roles.
The film tackles social issues like corporate exploitation of farmers and revolves around a battle between a petty thief and a powerful businessman.
With its strong message, engaging screenplay, and commercial success, Kaththi became a significant hit.

Rating: 4.3/5
