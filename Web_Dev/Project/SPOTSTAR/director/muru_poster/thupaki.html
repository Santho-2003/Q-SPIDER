SPOTSTAR - THUPAKI MOVIE

"Thuppakki" is a 2012 Tamil action thriller directed by A.R. Murugadoss, starring Vijay as an army officer on vacation in Mumbai.
The film follows his mission to track down and dismantle a terrorist sleeper cell, blending intense action with a gripping narrative.
Thuppakki was a major commercial success and is widely regarded as one of Vijay's most iconic films.

Rating: 4.3/5
