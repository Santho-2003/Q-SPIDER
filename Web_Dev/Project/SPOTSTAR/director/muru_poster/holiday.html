SPOTSTAR - HOLIDAY MOVIE

"Holiday: A Soldier Is Never Off Duty" is a 2014 Bollywood action thriller directed by A.R. Murugadoss.
The film stars Akshay Kumar as an army officer who fights against a sleeper cell terrorist group while on vacation.
Known for its gripping storyline and intense action sequences, the movie was both a critical and commercial success.

Rating: 4.3/5
