SPOTSTAR - SARKAR MOVIE

"Sarkar" is a 2018 Tamil political thriller directed by A.R. Murugadoss, starring Vijay as a successful businessman who returns to India to cast his vote,
only to find his vote has been illegally cast by someone else.
The film addresses issues of electoral fraud and the importance of individual votes, leading the protagonist to take on the corrupt political system.
With its powerful message and strong performances, Sarkar became a major commercial hit.

Rating: 4.3/5
