SPOTSTAR ATLEE MOVIES

Atlee is a prominent Indian film director and screenwriter known for his work in Tamil cinema.
He gained recognition with his debut film Raja Rani (2013) and went on to direct blockbuster hits like Theri (2016),
Mersal (2017), and Bigil (2019), all starring Vijay.
Atlee is known for blending commercial elements with strong emotional narratives, making him one of the most successful directors in contemporary Tamil cinema.
