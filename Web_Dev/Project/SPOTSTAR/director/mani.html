SPOTSTAR MANIRATHINAM MOVIES

Mani Ratnam is a legendary Indian filmmaker and screenwriter known for his groundbreaking work in Tamil cinema and Bollywood.
Renowned for his visually stunning and emotionally rich films, he has directed classics like Roja, Bombay, and Guru, often addressing complex social and political themes.
Mani Ratnam's innovative storytelling and directorial style have made him one of India's most respected and influential filmmakers.
