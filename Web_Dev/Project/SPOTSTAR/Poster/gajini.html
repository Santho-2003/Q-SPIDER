SPOTSTAR - GAJINI MOVIE

Ghajini (2005) is a Tamil psychological thriller starring Suriya in a dual role.
Directed by A.R. Murugadoss, the film follows the story of a man with short-term memory loss who is on a quest to avenge his girlfriend's murder.
Suriya's powerful performance, especially in portraying the emotional and physical challenges of his character, was widely praised.
The film became a major success, leading to remakes in other languages and solidifying Suriya's position as a top actor in Indian cinema.

Rating: 4.5/5
