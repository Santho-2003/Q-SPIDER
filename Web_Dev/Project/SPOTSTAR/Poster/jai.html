SPOTSTAR - JAI MOVIE

Jai (2004) is a Tamil action drama film starring Suriya in the lead role.
Directed by S. Narayan, the movie revolves around Jai, a young man who becomes a vigilante to protect his village from political corruption and injustice.
Suriya's intense performance as the fearless and determined protagonist was a highlight of the film.
The movie also featured a strong emotional core, focusing on themes of patriotism and social responsibility.
While Jai received mixed reviews, Suriya's acting and the film's message resonated with many viewers.

Rating: 4.3/5
