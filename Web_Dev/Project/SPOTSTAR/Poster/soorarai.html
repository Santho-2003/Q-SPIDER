SPOTSTAR - SOORARAI POTRU MOVIE

Soorarai Pottru (2020) is a critically acclaimed Tamil film starring Suriya, directed by Sudha Kongara.
The movie is inspired by the life of Captain G. R. Gopinath, the founder of India's low-cost airline Air Deccan.
Suriya plays the role of Maara, a determined man who dreams of making air travel affordable for the common man. The film highlights his struggles against the aviation industry's challenges and societal norms.
Praised for its powerful performances, especially by Suriya, and its inspiring storyline, Soorarai Pottru received numerous awards and became one of the highest-rated Indian films.

Rating: 4.9/5
