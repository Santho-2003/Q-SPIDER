SPOTSTAR - ANJAN MOVIE

Suriya starred in Anjaan (2014), a Tamil romantic action movie directed by N. Lingusamy.
In the film, Suriya plays dual roles as Raju Bhai, a feared gangster, and Krishna, his innocent brother.
The romantic subplot involves Raju Bhai's love interest, played by Samantha Ruth Prabhu, and their chemistry adds a lighter, romantic touch to the otherwise intense narrative.
The film blends action with romance, making it a unique mix of genres.
*Anjaan* was appreciated for its stylish visuals and Suriya's charismatic performance in both the romantic and action-packed scenes.


Rating: 4.9/5
